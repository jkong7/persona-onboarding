# Persona (yourpersona.com): company, product, and current onboarding

Research date: 2026-09-27. Read-only research. I did not text the Persona number, sign up, or enter any personal information, so the live onboarding conversation itself was NOT observed first-hand. Everything about the actual in-conversation flow is marked as unverified or inferred.

Verification legend used below:
- VERIFIED-PRIMARY = I read it on yourpersona.com (rendered page or raw HTML/JSON-LD).
- VERIFIED-SECONDARY = I read it in a third-party article that I fetched.
- SNIPPET-ONLY = seen only as a search-result title/snippet; the underlying page (X/Twitter) returned HTTP 402 and could not be opened.

Disambiguation: this is NOT withpersona.com (Persona Identities, Inc., identity verification, San Francisco, founded 2018). It is also not persona.ai (humanoid robots), yourpersona.ai, usepersona.app, the "Persona AI: Character Chat" Android app, or the New Jersey post-hardcore band "Your Persona" on Bandcamp. All of those surfaced in searches and are unrelated. Crunchbase / CB Insights "Persona AI" profiles that surfaced appear to be the robotics company and were not used.

## What does the agent do after onboarding? Which channels?

### Takeaway
After setup the assistant acts on the user's behalf across email, calendar, phone calls, messaging, files, web browsing and purchases, reached via iMessage/SMS today, with AI voice calls, a web dashboard, and the Band as additional surfaces; irreversible or money-spending actions wait for user approval.

### Cited Findings
- Terms describe Persona as "an AI assistant that acts on your behalf" covering email management, calendar operations, phone calls, messaging, file searches, web browsing and transactions where authorized. (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [Persona Terms](https://yourpersona.com/terms)
- Channels named in the Terms: "text messaging (including SMS and iMessage)," telephone calls, and web-based services. (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [Persona Terms](https://yourpersona.com/terms)
- Homepage has "Log in to the dashboard" linking to app.yourpersona.com, so a web app exists. (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com](https://yourpersona.com)
- Persona places outbound AI voice calls to third parties and to the user: users "expressly consent to receive calls from Persona at telephone numbers you provide, including calls using an artificial or AI-generated voice." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [Persona Terms](https://yourpersona.com/terms)
- Call records: "A transcript of every call is created and stored; audio recording occurs only where you enable it." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [Persona Terms](https://yourpersona.com/terms)
- Approval model (FAQ, verbatim): "It's proactive, so it spots what needs doing and prepares it before you ask. But the final call is yours. Anything that spends money, sends words in your name, or can't be undone waits for your yes. On the Band, that yes is one word." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Receipts (FAQ, verbatim): "Every task comes back with its receipts: the steps taken, the confirmation numbers, what was said on the call. When something can't be done, Persona says so and tells you what it needs, instead of pretending. That's the difference between an assistant and a chatbot." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Agent definition (FAQ, verbatim): "Persona is an agent with a personal mandate. It acts for you, in your name, with your approval, and it reports back with receipts." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)

### Inferences
- Gmail is the first and most important connection because "The secretary" role (read, draft, ask only when it matters) depends on it, and Gmail is listed first among integrations. This explains why Gmail is one of the four required onboarding fields in the take-home.
- The product's trust contract is "prepare proactively, act only on yes, report with receipts." An onboarding that mirrors this (confirm what it heard, state what it will do next, never claim a connection that did not happen) would be on-brand.

### Gaps
- Android / non-iMessage support is not stated on the site. The entry link uses the sms: scheme, so it may fall back to SMS, but this is unverified.

## What is known about the current onboarding (steps, call, names, Gmail, tone)?

### Takeaway
Publicly, Persona markets itself as having no onboarding at all: the only verified step is a web page that opens Messages with a pre-filled text to a Persona phone number. The in-conversation sequence (agent naming, the phone call, user name, Gmail connect, use case) is NOT publicly documented anywhere I could find; the take-home brief is the best available description of it.

### Cited Findings
- /start page copy, verbatim: heading "Start texting your Persona"; body "Persona lives in your messages. Send the first text and we'll take it from there."; button "Continue with iMessage"; link "Back to yourpersona.com". Page title "Persona - opening Messages". (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/start](https://yourpersona.com/start)
- The button is an SMS deep link to +1 (650) 285-8081 with the pre-filled message "Hey, what's a persona?". No form fields, no email field, no Gmail step, and no mention of a call on this page. (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/start](https://yourpersona.com/start)
- Site FAQ on setup, verbatim: "No prompts, no settings, no onboarding. Text it, or say it, the way you'd say it to a person. Persona learns your tone, your priorities and the people in your life as you go. The Band pairs once and stays paired." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Site FAQ on usage, verbatim: "Two ways. Today: open Messages, text Persona, that's the whole setup." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Band page copy: "Talk naturally and let your Persona handle the rest. No prompts to learn, nothing to set up." and "Talk to it the way you'd talk to your friend." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Users consent in the Terms to receive AI-voice calls from Persona at numbers they provide, which is the legal basis for an onboarding call to the user. (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [Persona Terms](https://yourpersona.com/terms)
- Other candidates' public take-home submissions exist and describe their own interpretations, not Persona's real flow:
  - amirlankalm/persona-onboarding: user names the agent on a blank screen, a contact card appears, the phone rings; accept starts a WebRTC call, decline falls to text chat; state machine naming -> ringing -> voice or text -> collecting -> graduated; marks Gmail as "address_provided" rather than claiming a connection. — [GitHub repo](https://github.com/amirlankalm/persona-onboarding)
  - coachv270/persona-onboarding PR 1 (merged September 26, 2026): "setup runs in the background and never gates requests"; UI styled after Persona as "light, SF Pro/Inter, pills, iMessage bubbles". — [GitHub PR](https://github.com/coachv270/persona-onboarding/pull/1)
  - momenbuilds/persona-onboard: "Voice-first onboarding for Persona: real-time voice call, DeepSeek understanding, and a dashboard chat". — [GitHub repo](https://github.com/momenbuilds/persona-onboard)

### Inferences
- The likely real order, reconstructed from the take-home wording plus the /start page, is: (1) user sends the first text from /start, (2) agent name is settled in text before the call (the brief says the call collects "everything except the agent name"), (3) Persona phones the user, (4) on the call it gathers the user's name and what they want help with, and arranges the Gmail connection, which cannot be completed by voice and so presumably goes out as a link in the text thread, (5) user lands in the normal thread. This ordering is an inference, not an observed fact.
- The take-home's constraint "shouldn't feel like a form" is a direct echo of the public promise "No prompts, no settings, no onboarding." The brand claim is that onboarding is invisible; the design target is a conversation that happens to collect four facts.
- "Graduate early" aligns with "You can hand it real tasks from day one" and with Persona learning "as you go": a user who opens with a real task should be served immediately and the missing fields collected later or in the background.
- The pre-filled opener "Hey, what's a persona?" means the first user message is a question, so the agent's first reply is an answer/introduction, not a request for data. Tone is set as casual and curious from message one.
- Multiple candidates have public repos for the same brief, so an approach that merely reproduces "name screen, ringing phone, decline to chat" is likely to look common. This is a competitive observation, not a fact about Persona.
- Candidate repos are useful only as a view of the field. They should not be treated as evidence of how Persona works, and nothing from them should be copied.

### Gaps
- The actual wording of Persona's onboarding messages, how it asks for names, whether the call is inbound to the user or user-initiated, call length, and the voice persona are all unknown. No demo video, transcript, or walkthrough was found.
- How Gmail is connected in the real product (OAuth link in thread vs dashboard at app.yourpersona.com) is not documented publicly. The Google limited-use language in the Privacy Policy implies Google OAuth, but the UX is unverified.
- Whether a default agent name exists, or whether naming is mandatory, is unknown.
- The founder's X post that surfaced about people "breaking down our onboarding flow" (status 1923852481886298453) dates from 2025 by its ID and concerns Cal AI, not Persona; it was not readable and is excluded as evidence.

## Brand and tone

### Takeaway
The voice is short, confident, plain-spoken and slightly irreverent, built on outcome verbs and the "capable friend" metaphor, paired with a clean light iMessage-style visual identity and a heavy privacy/trust counterweight.

### Cited Findings
- Irreverent headline on /band: "First AI assistant you can wear. Made to get sh*t done." The homepage uses the cleaner variant "Made to get it done." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band), [yourpersona.com](https://yourpersona.com)
- Friend framing: "Text it in iMessage like you'd text a capable friend"; "Talk to it the way you'd talk to your friend." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Short paired-sentence rhythm: "Yours to delete. Gone means gone."; "Never sold, never traded. Not a product, not to anyone."; "Your entire digital life, handled." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com](https://yourpersona.com)
- Honesty as a brand value: "When something can't be done, Persona says so and tells you what it needs, instead of pretending." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Role copy uses assertive, human verbs: "argues the fee", "chases late payments", "argues with the airline, and gets the money back". (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Personalization promise: "It picks up your way of doing things and sticks to it."; "Persona learns your tone, your priorities and the people in your life as you go." (VERIFIED-PRIMARY) — [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- Site assets reference an iMessage hero graphic, an iOS keyboard graphic and an iPhone 17 Pro frame, i.e. the product is presented inside an iPhone Messages thread. (VERIFIED-PRIMARY, from page asset paths) — [yourpersona.com](https://yourpersona.com)
- A candidate who studied the site characterized the visual style as "light, SF Pro/Inter, pills, iMessage bubbles". (VERIFIED-SECONDARY, third-party opinion) — [GitHub PR](https://github.com/coachv270/persona-onboarding/pull/1)

### Inferences
- An on-brand onboarding agent would speak in short sentences, avoid assistant boilerplate, be direct about what it can and cannot do, and sound like a competent friend rather than a concierge or a form.
- A simulator that looks like an iPhone Messages thread plus a native-looking incoming call screen would match the brand better than a generic chat widget.
- Swearing is used in marketing copy but softened on the homepage, so mild informality is safe while profanity in the agent's own voice is a risk.

### Gaps
- No brand guidelines, font names, or color tokens were confirmed from a primary source.
- The agent's real conversational voice (in text or on calls) was not observed.

## Public user feedback and complaints

### Takeaway
The product is about two weeks past public launch and I found almost no independent user feedback; what exists is press skepticism about AI wearables and about the risk of an agent with credentials, a voice and spending authority.

### Cited Findings
- Risk framing: "An agent with stored credentials, a telephone voice and permission to spend money can create obligations before the user notices the mistake." (VERIFIED-SECONDARY) — [Runtime Wire](https://runtimewire.com/article/zach-yadegari-persona-ai-band-launch)
- Terms treat agent actions as the user's own, including misunderstood instructions. (VERIFIED-SECONDARY, consistent with primary Terms quote) — [Runtime Wire](https://runtimewire.com/article/zach-yadegari-persona-ai-band-launch), [Persona Terms](https://yourpersona.com/terms)
- Privacy caveat reported by press: company says it does not sell data or train on connected content, but "third-party AI providers may use data for model or service improvement, including training." (VERIFIED-SECONDARY) — [The Gadgeteer](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/09/17/persona-band-ai-wristband-preview/)
- Wearable skepticism: Stuff questions whether Persona will "prove a more positive experience" than Humane AI Pin and Rabbit, noting similar companies made claims they "never managed to pull off." (VERIFIED-SECONDARY) — [Stuff](https://stuff.co.za/2026/09/18/persona-band-another-shot-at-wearable-ai/)
- The Gadgeteer flags unproven preorder-only hardware, multi-step task errors, and the chance of contacting the wrong person or making unintended transactions. (VERIFIED-SECONDARY) — [The Gadgeteer](https://the-gadgeteer.com/2026/09/17/persona-band-ai-wristband-preview/)
- Dealroom states pre-orders have "no announced shipping date", which conflicts with the site's December 2026 date. — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/154060-persona-ai-raises-5m-for-a-wrist-bracelet-that-acts-on-your-voice/); contradicted by [yourpersona.com/band](https://yourpersona.com/band)
- One positive anecdote from Kevin Hartz about Persona calling his wife and booking a dinner reservation. (SNIPPET-ONLY) — [X post](https://x.com/kevinhartz/status/2099936408777703469)

### Inferences
- The main public objection is trust, not capability. An onboarding that is explicit about permissions (what Gmail access is for, that nothing is sent without a yes) addresses the most cited concern.
- Because the Terms obtain consent for AI-voice calls, a respectful onboarding should still make the call feel expected (announce it in text first) rather than surprising the user.

### Gaps
- No App Store reviews, Reddit threads, Hacker News threads, or Product Hunt comments found.
- No complaints specific to onboarding, the call, or Gmail connection were found.
- Replies to the launch posts on X were not readable.
- Whether Kevin Hartz is an investor is unknown; his post should be read as potentially non-independent.
